# FoodVerse AI — Engineering & Scientific Limitations

Transparent disclosure of system boundaries and scientific limitations is a mandatory tenet of FoodVerse AI. This document outlines key technical, legal, and safety boundaries.

---

## 1. Food Recognition & Nutrition Estimation Limitations

1. **Volume and Mass Ambiguity:** 2D computer vision images inherently lack physical depth and density sensors. Portion weights are estimated based on typical container dimensions and standard serving sizes. Real mass may vary significantly based on moisture content, culinary preparation, and hidden fats/oils.
2. **Hidden Ingredients:** Commercial and restaurant meals frequently contain emulsified oils, hidden sugars, salt, and thickeners that leave no visual trace on the surface. Nutritional calculations reflect standard recipe preparations rather than exact chemical assays.
3. **Class Overlaps:** Structurally similar foods (e.g., cauliflower mash vs. mashed potatoes, almond milk vs. dairy milk) may have low distinction in visual models without contextual user prompts.

---

## 2. Food Freshness & Microbiological Safety Boundaries

1. **Invisible Pathogens:** The most dangerous foodborne illness agents—including *Salmonella enterica*, *Escherichia coli*, *Listeria monocytogenes*, and *Clostridium botulinum*—produce no early visual degradation, odor, or surface discoloration.
2. **Optical Limitations:** High-resolution cameras can identify surface fungal mycelium, enzymatic browning, tissue weeping, and structural collapse, but cannot evaluate internal core temperatures or bacterial colony counts.
3. **Disclaimer:** FoodVerse AI outputs visual freshness assessments only. It is strictly never a microbiological safety certifier. When any perishable food displays abnormal odor, texture, or has exceeded safe temperature windows, it must be discarded immediately.

---

## 3. Restaurant Hygiene Video Analysis Boundaries

1. **Blind Spots and Camera Angles:** Fixed or mobile camera feeds cannot see beneath countertops, inside closed refrigeration units, or areas obstructed by kitchen personnel.
2. **Chemical Sanitization Verification:** Computer vision can observe surface wiping actions, but cannot quantify the concentration of quaternary ammonium or chlorine sanitizer in the wipe bucket.
3. **Non-Regulatory Status:** Automated hygiene audits provide assistive training and internal quality benchmarks. They do not constitute official statutory inspections under FDA Food Code or local public health authority statutes.

---

## 4. Cooking Assistance and Meal Planning Boundaries

1. **Not Clinical Nutrition Therapy:** Meal plans generated by FoodVerse AI are designed for general wellness and lifestyle organization. They are not intended as medical nutrition therapy for acute diseases (such as chronic kidney disease, uncontrolled type 1 diabetes, or inborn errors of metabolism).
2. **Severe Anaphylactic Allergies:** While the meal planner rigorously eliminates requested allergens from recipes and shopping lists, cross-contact and commercial ingredient supply variations necessitate that individuals with life-threatening allergies verify commercial packaging labels directly.
